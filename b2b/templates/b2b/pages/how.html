{% t "How it works" %}

Kubbar sells batches that are within six months of expiry, after first offering them to existing customers. Every batch is registered for the Egyptian market and has at least 30 days of shelf life left.

1

{% t "Register" %}

Create a company account with your commercial register, tax card, the owner's national ID and your licence to trade veterinary products.

2

{% t "Get verified" %}

Our Regulatory Affairs team checks the documents and sets the categories you may buy, and whether you can receive cold-chain products.

3

{% t "Order" %}

See prices, add batches to your cart and check out. Stock is reserved for you for 48 hours while you pay by bank transfer, or on invoice if you have credit terms.

4

{% t "Receive" %}

Once Finance confirms payment we pick the exact lot you bought and dispatch it. Follow every step in My Orders and download your invoice.

Ready to start?
Registration takes about 10 minutes.
